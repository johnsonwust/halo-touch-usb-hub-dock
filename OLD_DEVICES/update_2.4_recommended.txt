Firmware 2.4 Update Strongly Recommended

1. Firmware 2.4 uses a new JPEG decoding library. It improves MJPEG playback frame rate by about 60% to 90% for God Eye, dynamic clock backgrounds, video playback, and photo albums.
2. Some small bugs are fixed.
3. The new decoder requires MJPEG and JPG files to match the device screen resolution, such as 240 x 240. Files with the wrong size may cause playback errors.
